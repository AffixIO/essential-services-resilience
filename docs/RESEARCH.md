# Research basis

Reviewed 3 November 2026. Sources are official guidance, used for scope rather than operational instruction.

- [UK Government Resilience Framework](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-uk-government-resilience-framework/the-uk-government-resilience-framework-html) identifies 13 CNI sectors and describes cascading disruption, including power loss affecting communications.
- [Electricity Supply Emergency Code, revised April 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/electricity-supply-emergency-code/electricity-supply-emergency-code) identifies the significance of essential sites such as drinking water and certain communication services during electricity emergencies.
- [NCSC guidance for CNI severe cyber threat](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/how-to-prepare-and-plan-your-organisations-response-to-severe-cyber-threat-a-guide-for-cni) emphasises planning, maintaining operations and recovery, and says the correct measures depend on the organisation.
- [NCSC guidance on diversity and dependencies](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/staticjson/ncsc-content/files/NIS%20Guidance%20Collection%201.0.pdf) notes offline backups, manual alternatives and the need to manage dependencies.
- [Government local resilience forum guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/local-resilience-forums-contact-details) describes the multi-agency resilience structure across the UK.

These sources do not endorse AffixIO or this repository. The implementation does not claim compliance with any resilience, cyber, safety or sector-specific standard. Its narrow inference is that preserving constrained, accountable records may be useful as one component of a wider human-led continuity plan.
